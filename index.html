Finlee Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Finlee is a personal finance app. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not. In short: your financial data belongs to you, we do not collect it, and we do not run analytics or advertising.

Data stored in the app

Books, wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, reports and other data you enter are stored on your device. We (the developer) have no access to this data and it is never sent to our servers, because we do not operate any.

iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud Sync (a Finlee Pro feature), your data is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and synced between your devices. It is protected by your Apple ID and governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see or access it.

Backups and exports

Backups and exported files (such as CSV) are created on your device, in the locations you choose. You decide where to store or share them.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rate files from third-party content delivery services. These requests contain no personal or financial data, but like any internet request they are visible to the service as your IP address.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you use the option to protect hidden amounts, the app asks the system to authenticate you. Biometric data is processed by iOS and macOS only and is never available to the app or to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active purchase.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

Finlee contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share your data.

Children

Finlee does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data at any time in Settings → Data → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. If iCloud Sync is on, data is also removed from your iCloud storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
